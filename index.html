Swahili AI Scorecard

Do AI models handle the Swahili Kenyans actually use? Twenty everyday prompts on proverbs, M-Pesa, county services, farming and code-switching. Run each prompt on a model, then score the answer here.

The board starts empty on purpose. Every score on it comes from a test you ran yourself.

How to score

AccuracyFacts, steps and meanings are correct. Nothing invented, such as fake fees or phone numbers.
FluencyGrammar, spelling and word choice read like a Swahili speaker wrote it.
Cultural fitFits Kenyan life: real services, natural register, Kenyan Swahili and Sheng where asked.

Each score runs from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Use the same prompt text on every model, in a new chat each time.
